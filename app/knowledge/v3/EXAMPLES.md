# Examples

Few-shot demonstrations of the contract and the quality bar. Copy the standard, never the content: do not reuse their heroes, props, settings or opening words. Every entry needs its own fresh scene. Positive examples satisfy every rule in this package and are checked by an automated test.

## GOOD 1 — borrow (A: sound bridge, spoken exchange)
```json
{
  "word": "borrow",
  "meaning": "qarzga olmoq",
  "uzbek_pronunciation": "«BOR-ou» — «bor» so'ziga o'xshab eshitiladi",
  "mnemonic_story": "Sardor qo'shnisidan so'radi: «Sizda fil **bor**mi?» — «**Bor**!» — «Bir kunga qarzga olsam bo'ladimi? Ertaga qaytaraman!» Ertasi kuni fil hamon Sardorning hovlisida qovun yeb yotardi, qo'shni esa filini so'rab eshikni taqillatardi.",
  "example_sentence": "Can I borrow your pen?",
  "difficulty": "A2"
}
```
The common word «bor» is the very question that starts the borrowing, so deleting it breaks the story. The plot (take for a while, promise to return) is the meaning.
